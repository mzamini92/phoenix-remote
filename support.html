Connecting a TV

Connect your iPhone and supported TV or streaming device to the same local network. Allow Local Network access in iPhone Settings. In Rooms, choose Add TV or streaming stick, select a discovered device, and complete any PIN or approval shown on the TV. Guest networks and network isolation can prevent discovery.

Available controls

Commands vary by device and TV app. A streaming stick may not support TV volume or power through its network interface. The current build includes Roku, Samsung, LG webOS, Fire TV, and Google Cast integrations; inclusion does not mean every model or function has been tested. Google Cast supports media casting rather than general Google TV navigation. Siri and Lock Screen controls currently use Roku. The Watch companion requires the paired iPhone for commands.

Subscriptions

Apple shows the applicable localized price, billing period, and any eligible trial before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TV Remote: Tap & Control to manage or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Use Restore on the app's subscription screen with the Apple Account used to purchase.

Remote access requires an active entitlement verified by Apple. When that entitlement ends, the subscription page returns and remote access is unavailable. Any Apple-recognized billing grace period is respected.
