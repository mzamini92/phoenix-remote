How can we help?

Support for Room Remote on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Contact support

phoenixremote.support@gmail.com

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, payment information, or pairing tokens.

Connecting a TV

Connect your iPhone and supported TV or streaming device to the same local network. Allow Local Network access in iPhone Settings. In Rooms, choose Add TV or streaming stick, select a discovered device, and complete any PIN or approval shown on the TV. Guest networks and network isolation can prevent discovery.

Available controls

Commands vary by device and TV app. A streaming stick may not support TV volume or power through its network interface. Siri and Lock Screen controls currently support Roku. The Watch companion requires the paired iPhone for commands.

Subscriptions

For cancellation or renewal settings, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Room Remote. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Use Restore on the app's subscription screen with the Apple Account used to purchase. Trial eligibility and local pricing appear in Apple's purchase sheet.
