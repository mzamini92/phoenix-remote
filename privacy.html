What the app does

TV Remote: Tap & Control controls supported TVs and streaming devices over your local network. No TV Remote: Tap & Control account is required. Apple handles subscription purchases. Supported functions vary by TV, streaming device, and TV app.

Device information and local storage

The app processes device names, room names, local network addresses, models and identifiers to discover, pair with, and control your devices. Saved devices, selected-device information, preferences, and custom TV-app ordering are stored locally. Installed TV-app names and identifiers are requested from supported devices to display and launch apps. Device icons may be requested from the TV. App-authored code does not upload your room profiles or installed TV-app list to a publisher-operated server. iOS backup settings may affect backup and restoration of local settings.

Pairing credentials and approved certificate fingerprints for supported devices are stored in iOS Keychain. Keychain records may remain after uninstall. Earlier development versions may also have saved preview-access records locally; these records do not grant subscription access in the release version.

Commands and text entry

Remote commands and text you enter are sent to your selected device. The TV and its apps may process this information under their own policies. Some local control protocols are unencrypted. Use a trusted network and avoid entering sensitive information into TV text fields.

Photos, videos, and media URLs

The app uses Apple's system photo picker to access only media you select. An iCloud item may first be downloaded by the system. For supported casting, the app prepares a temporary JPEG or MP4 and serves the prepared file over local HTTP using a randomly generated URL. This local transfer is not encrypted. Someone who obtains that active URL and can reach your iPhone could access the shared file. The app does not upload selected media to a publisher-operated server.

Stopping sharing, leaving the casting view, backgrounding the app, or disconnecting normally stops local serving and removes app-created temporary copies. Unexpected termination may leave temporary files until cleanup. Stopping sharing cannot erase data already received by a receiver. If you enter a media URL, the receiver may request it from its hosting service, which can receive network addresses and information included in that URL.

Apple Watch, Lock Screen, and Siri

The Watch companion exchanges device names and identifiers, TV-app lists, selected-device and connection information, and commands with the paired iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. Optional Lock Screen controls store local session and device-routing information. Device names and status may appear on the Lock Screen or Watch Smart Stack.

Siri and Shortcuts actions use the selected Roku and can request its installed-app list. Apple handles Siri voice processing under its policies. TV Remote: Tap & Control does not record microphone audio. Commands are sent over the local network after checking subscription access.

Subscriptions and payments

Apple processes purchases using your Apple Account. TV Remote: Tap & Control does not receive your payment-card number. Apple's StoreKit supplies product and transaction information needed to verify and restore access, including subscription status. The app verifies Apple-signed transactions on the device and does not send them to a publisher-operated billing server. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility; reinstalling does not reset eligibility for the same account and subscription group.

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Manage or cancel it through your Apple Account subscriptions. Apple retains purchase records according to Apple's privacy policy.

Third-party services

The app includes Google's Cast SDK and networking dependencies for discovery, sessions, and casting. Google discloses SDK diagnostic processing, including coarse-location and performance data. The app disables the SDK's analytics-logging option and uses its own device picker; this does not guarantee that all Google diagnostics are disabled. Google's processing and retention are governed by Google's privacy policy.

TV manufacturers, streaming apps, media hosts, and Apple may independently process information when you use their services. The app has no advertising SDK, publisher analytics service, publisher account-registration server, or publisher cloud-storage service. It does not implement cross-app advertising tracking.

Permissions

Local Network permission allows discovery and device communication. You can revoke it in iPhone Settings, which limits remote functionality. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or full photo-library access. The system photo picker limits access to selected items.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved device profiles and preferences remain until changed, removed, or local app data is deleted. Swipe left on a paired device in Rooms to remove its profile. Removing a profile does not necessarily erase historical Keychain pairing credentials. Revoke the app's permission on the TV to prevent future access; contact support if you need help locating that setting. Third parties retain their own records under their policies.

If you email support, Mohamad Zamini and Google's Gmail service process the information you send to answer your request. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, follow-up, disputes, and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email; records that must be retained for legal reasons may be excluded. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or pairing tokens.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restrictions concerning personal information the publisher holds. Email the privacy contact to make a request. Device-local information can often be managed on your device. Requests about information independently held by Apple, Google, or TV services should be directed to those providers.
