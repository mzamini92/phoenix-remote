Your privacy choices

These options are available whether or not you have an active subscription.

Local network access

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network to change the app's permission. Turning it off limits discovery and TV control.

Saved devices and pairing

Remove saved device profiles from Rooms when that screen is accessible. Removing a profile or uninstalling the app may not erase Keychain pairing records. Revoke the app's access in your TV's approved-device settings. Contact support if you cannot reach the relevant app controls or need help finding the TV setting. We cannot remotely erase records stored only on your iPhone or TV.

Photos and videos

Select only the media you want to share. Stop sharing or leave the casting view to stop local serving. This does not remove copies already received by a TV or receiver.

Apple diagnostic sharing

Review Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone, including Share With App Developers where available. These controls govern Apple's sharing, not Google Cast's independent processing. Learn more in Apple's notice.

Google Cast

This app disables the Cast SDK's analytics-logging option. It has no separate in-app switch that guarantees all Cast diagnostic processing stops. Avoid initiating Cast features if you do not want to use that service; this is not a guarantee that all SDK processing is prevented. For information Google independently holds, review Google's privacy controls and policy.

Ask about your information

Email phoenixremote.support@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence we hold, or to ask a privacy question. Describe your request and use the email address you previously contacted us from where possible. Do not send identity documents, passwords, payment details, or pairing tokens unless a specific need and secure method have been agreed.

We respond according to applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or disputes. Apple, Google, and other providers manage their own records; we cannot promise to delete information held independently by them.

Subscription choices

Privacy requests and deleting the app do not cancel subscriptions. Manage your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
